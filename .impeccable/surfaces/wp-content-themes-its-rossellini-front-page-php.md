# Home (front page) — ITS Academy Roberto Rossellini

Scope: WordPress front page of the classic theme `its-rossellini`; the same system carries every other template. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: an 18–22-year-old (and family) on a phone deciding whether a two-year, publicly funded audiovisual course is worth it, and which one. Action: book a visit (open day or individual meeting) or pre-enrol. Proof on hand: 8 real courses with real photos, the real hero video, published figures (87% employed within 12 months, 1,000 hours, 800+ hours internship, 28 places), the 360 m² sound stage in Via Tiburtina, funding bodies, partner logos. Constraints: logo, institutional palette (blue, deep blue, cyan, electric blue; no yellow/green/red) and Raleway are binding; the current site is the reference to stay faithful to; no invented claims; no content hidden by scroll animation; Polylang IT/EN.

Superseded: the earlier "locandina e fotobusta" direction (yellow second ink, area colours, condensed face) was rejected by the user as not modern and not on-brand.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The institution's site in today's product-site language the user pointed to: rounded panels on a deep-navy canvas, glass over real footage, light display type. It refuses both the current site's film-tech HUD costume and the flat corporate template.

OWN-WORLD: Canvas in deep-navy tones of the institutional blue (#04152b, #0a2140); white light panels with a soft cyan glow; cyan as emphasis on dark, electric blue on light; electric-to-blue gradient for primary buttons and the accent tile. Raleway 300 for display in sentence case with one phrase at 600; 22–40px radii; glass pills and cards (blur 14–18px); pill buttons with a rotating arrow.

STORY: The visitor lands on the footage inside a rounded card, reads "Il futuro si gira adesso.", sees the 87% figure and where the school is, and can book a visit. Below: partners, a bento of figures and photos about the open set, the eight courses as image cards, one comparison card of what the biennio includes, the path from guidance to work, where the school is (chips + globe, Erasmus+), news, the six most asked questions, and a gradient call to visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rounded hero card inset 8–16px from the viewport, video under a left-weighted navy scrim; floating pill header (logo, glass nav pill, white "Preiscriviti"); headline bottom-left up to ~7.25rem with "si gira" in cyan 600; lead; white "Prenota un incontro ↗" and glass "Scopri i corsi"; bottom-right two glass cards (87%, visit/next event) and the pause control; white partner bar overlapping the card's lower edge.

FORM: user-supplied references (three Dribbble shots, then deluxhost.net for the comparison card, place block, FAQ and footer) translated into the institutional palette and Raleway; seed: none (user-pinned references). Signature interaction: the header pill turning into a glass bar on scroll; arrows rotating to ↗ on hover across buttons and cards.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
