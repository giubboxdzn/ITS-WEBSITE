# Home (front page) — ITS Academy Roberto Rossellini

Scope: WordPress front page of the new classic theme `its-rossellini`; the same world carries every other template. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: an 18–22-year-old (and family) on a phone deciding whether a two-year, publicly funded audiovisual course is worth it, and which one. Action: book an open day / individual guidance, or pre-enrol. Proof on hand: 8 real courses with real photos, published figures (87% employed within 12 months, 1,000 hours, 800+ hours internship, 28 places), the 360 m² sound stage in Via Tiburtina, funding bodies, partner logos. Constraints: logo and institutional blue are binding; no invented claims; no content hidden by scroll animation; Polylang IT/EN.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the cinema's printed paper, locandina and fotobuste, not a "film-tech" interface. It refuses the academy template (dark video hero, stat band, equal course cards, HUD labels) and the incumbent's timecode/REC costume.

OWN-WORLD: Two inks on white poster stock: institutional blue (#004590, deep #002c64) as dominant field, chrome yellow as the second ink. Photography printed in bicromia (duotone) with one fixed ink per technological area, inside fotobusta frames: white margin, photo, title band. Display type is a self-hosted extra-condensed gothic in caps; text is its regular-width sibling. Facts set as a poster billing block. Rules are printed lines, no shadows, no glows, no rounded cards. Dated items carry a cinema programme stamp (Prossimamente / Oggi / Ultimi giorni / Chiuso) computed from real dates.

STORY: Within one screen the visitor reads what this is (a free two-year academy for cinema, TV, digital, live and tech jobs in Rome), sees the real set, and can act. Scrolling, they pick a course like choosing a film from the lobby wall, see the stage, the 87% figure as a sentence, the three phases of the biennium, what's on now, and who backs it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed blue field. Top: logo (white) and nav. Left two-thirds on desktop: the title "Il futuro si gira adesso." set huge in yellow/white gothic caps, one supporting line, primary action "Prenota un incontro" (yellow) and secondary "Scopri i corsi". Right: a portrait fotobusta photo (duotone blue) of the lab/set, white margin. Bottom band across the viewport: billing block (2 anni · 1.000 ore · 800+ ore di stage · corsi finanziati · Via Tiburtina 691) and the programme stamp of the next open day when one exists. Mobile: title, actions, then photo; billing block below.

FORM: Italian cinema poster and lobby-card print (locandina e fotobusta), position 6 of the grounded list; seed:05ad693a9bcb9daf. Raises kept: dates always in the same slot on every dated item (from the seven-segment challenger's fixed-cell discipline); chronology as depth only on the news wall, never on courses (from the sticker-accretion challenger), rendered as scale: the newest bill prints largest on top of the stack. Signature interaction: on load the yellow plate prints a few pixels out of register and settles into register (once, reduced-motion safe); course filter swaps the lobby wall by area without hiding content when JS is off.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
