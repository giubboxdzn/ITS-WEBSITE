# Home (front page) — ITS Academy Roberto Rossellini

Scope: WordPress front page of the classic theme `its-rossellini`; the same system carries every other template. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: an 18–22-year-old (and family) on a phone deciding whether a two-year, publicly funded audiovisual course is worth it, and which one. Action: book a visit (open day or individual meeting) or pre-enrol. Proof on hand: 8 real courses with real photos, the real hero video, published figures (87% employed within 12 months, 1,000 hours, 800+ hours internship, 28 places), the 360 m² sound stage in Via Tiburtina, funding bodies, partner logos. Constraints: logo, institutional palette (blue, deep blue, cyan, electric blue; no yellow/green/red) and Raleway are binding; the current site is the reference to stay faithful to; no invented claims; no content hidden by scroll animation; Polylang IT/EN.

Superseded: the earlier "locandina e fotobusta" direction (yellow second ink, area colours, condensed face) was rejected by the user as not modern and not on-brand.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The current Rossellini site, grown up: same institution, palette, Raleway voice, video and structure, rebuilt with modern restraint. It refuses the "film-tech HUD" costume that makes the current site read as AI-made (mono micro-labels, counters, kickers, grids, glows, camera frames, stripes, ticker).

OWN-WORLD: Deep blue and blue fields alternating with white and a cool light grey; cyan as the single accent, on dark grounds and as solid fills. Raleway only: 900 uppercase headlines with tight but legible tracking and an italic second line in cyan (on dark) or blue (on light); 400 body with lining numerals. Real photographs in rounded 14px frames, rounded cards with a hairline border, pill buttons in sentence case, circular arrow buttons on list rows. No shadows beyond a soft lift on card hover, no gradients except the video's legibility scrim, no glows.

STORY: In the first screen the visitor sees students at work in the real video, reads "Il futuro si gira adesso." and can act (book a meeting, see courses). Scrolling: the eight courses filterable by area, the open set (stage and labs), the 87% sentence and the three steps of the path, what's new, who backs it, and a final call to visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hero video (poster image when motion is reduced or data saving is on) under a deep-blue scrim. Transparent header over it with white logo, nav and a cyan "Preiscriviti" pill; it turns solid deep blue on scroll. Bottom-left: headline in three lines at up to ~8rem, "si gira" italic cyan; one-sentence lead; primary cyan pill "Prenota un incontro" and outline pill "Scopri i corsi". Bottom-right: a round pause/play control for the video.

FORM: the incumbent itsrossellini.it theme, modernised; direction pinned by the user, no concept roll for this round (seed: none (user-pinned incumbent world)). Signature interaction: the header settling from transparent to solid as the video scrolls away; the course filter swaps cards in place and works as plain links without JS.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
